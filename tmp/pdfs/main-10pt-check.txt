1516   ActDelta tests a plausible correction to predictive transmis-                                                                                   1569
1517   sion for edge-offloaded robot policies: schedule uploads by                                                                                     1570
1518   their effect on policy actions rather than by reconstruction                                                                                    1571

1520   rics weakly rank action divergence, while a latent decision                                                                                     1573
1521   head reaches Spearman 0.684 and AUROC 0.833. It does not                                                                                        1574
1522   succeed as a demonstrated scheduler. Under frozen, matched-                                                                                     1575
1523   budget evaluation across a full budget sweep, the learned                                                                                       1576
1524   trigger does not establish an advantage over periodic sam-                                                                                      1577
1525   pling; an oracle using true action divergence also fails; and a                                                                                 1578
1526   content-blind Bernoulli scheduler at the same mean rate is                                                                                      1579
1527   not separated from the adaptive arms by an experiment sized                                                                                     1580
1528   to resolve the difference between adaptive and fidelity-based                                                                                   1581
